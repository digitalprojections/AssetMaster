Feature request privacy

Feature requests send your title, description and optional reply email to the AssetMaster team for evaluating product ideas and responding to your request. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. Projects, images and device files are not attached.

Requests are delivered through Resend and the receiving email provider. Submissions remain private and are not published or used for marketing. The team keeps correspondence only while needed to review and follow up on the request; you can request deletion by replying to a response.

The server uses a temporary hash of your network address to limit spam. These records expire after one hour and are cleared on subsequent submissions. Request text is not stored in these records.
